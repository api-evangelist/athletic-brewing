For years, non-alcoholic beer was seen as a concession, or worse, a punch line in a bad joke. They were often unexciting beverages relegated to designated drivers, people avoiding alcohol for medical reasons, or those in recovery. Even as today’s NA beer landscape feels vast and limitless, that outdated stigma still lingers.
