Learn how to pair a variety of non-alcoholic styles with everything from barbecue to dessert. Non-alcoholic beer can be paired with food using the same principles that make traditional beer pairings work: complementing flavors, creating contrast and refreshing your palate between bites. The absence of alcohol doesn’t mean the absence of malt, hops, acidity, bitterness, aroma or body.
