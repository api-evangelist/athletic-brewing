New York just got a new game day ritual. Athletic Brewing Company has entered a new multi-year partnership with MetLife Stadium, becoming a Proud Non-Alcoholic Beer Partner of the Stadium, the New York Jets, and the New York Giants. It's a first-of-its-kind milestone: Athletic's first sponsorship of two professional football teams and a their home stadium, and the first time MetLife Stadium and its home teams have partnered with a dedicated non-alcoholic brewer.
