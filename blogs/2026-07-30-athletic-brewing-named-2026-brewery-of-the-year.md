We have some pretty big news: Athletic Brewing was named 2026 Brewery of the Year at the third annual International Non-Alcoholic Competition. Our lineup also earned eight individual medals, including one Double Gold, three Golds, two Silvers and two Bronzes. We are proud of the recognition.
