Trevor’s desire to create surfboards started back in 2020 after the breakout of the Covid-19 pandemic sent college students home for a semester. Welcome to Mastering the Craft– the ultimate fusion of non-alcoholic beer and one of our teammates living the Athletic Brewing lifestyle with an NA beer by his side. Life now might not have as many pre-dawn visits to the beach, but there are new ways to remind him of life among the waves.
